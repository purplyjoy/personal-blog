Fading Friendship

It feels different when you're no longer in constant communication with someone who used to be such a big part of your everyday life. Everything felt a little off when I stopped talking to my closest online friend. Even the games we used to play together didn't feel the same anymore. I started losing interest in playing them because they reminded me of her. She was one of my favorite people, and our friendship became something I really valued. We talked almost everyday, shared random thoughts, played together, and somehow close enough that I thought we'd stay friends for a long time.

Everything I felt or experienced, I used to share with her. Whether it was something funny, something frustrating, or just a random thought, she was always the person I wanted to tell. But now, I don't have her to share and laugh about those little things anymore. She was the only friend I felt that deeply connected to. She was also the person I became the most open and vulnerable with. I didn't have to think twice about sharing things with her because, somehow, I felt comfortable enough to let my guard down.

But things changed after I tried to talk to her about something I was genuinely worried about. I knew I might have been too involved, but I couldn't just ignore something that I felt was hurting her. I tried telling her what I thought because I care about her and wanted her to be okay. Still, she has her own choices to make, and I eventually realized that I can't decide things for her. I tried to distance myself from the situation, but it was harder than I expected. I cared too much to simply pretend that everything was fine, yet staying involved was also becoming exhausting. Eventually, I felt like stepping away was the only thing I could do. I don't know if I made the right choice. Maybe I should have stayed out of it from the beginning. Maybe I cared too much. But I know I tried, I really did want the best for her.

Now, everything just feels different. It's strange how someone can become such a familiar part of your routine and then suddenly not be there anymore. I don't think I was prepared for how much I'd notice her absence. I guess some friendships can become part of us faster than we expect, and somehow, even when we don't want them to, they change.
